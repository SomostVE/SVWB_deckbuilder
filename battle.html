This is the first rules-engine pass. Basic battle flow, followers, field/hand limits, PP, Ward/Rush/Storm/Bane/Drain, evolution, super-evolution and a small set of simple text effects are modeled. Complex card-specific mechanics are explicitly counted as unsupported instead of being treated as accurate.
